Chewdash Taxi

Live

Local rides, food pickup & snack delivery

PROJECT 02 / 2026

Ponte

Live

Live-translation video calls

Ponte grew out of a straightforward need: helping people maintain a close connection across distance when they speak different languages. A conversation should have room for spontaneity, even when the words do not come easily. The video-call app is live and used daily.

THE APPROACH

Translated captions appear during the call, keeping translation alongside the person speaking. The interface supports a continuing conversation rather than making translation a separate task. A static frontend and a separate Express API provide the application’s two main pieces.

JavaScript / Express / Vercel / Railway

PROJECT 03 / 2026

KnowYourLocal

Alpha

Nationwide local-services marketplace

KnowYourLocal started with the idea of a peer-to-peer handyman marketplace. People need someone nearby they can trust with a job; local workers need a way to get discovered and find work. Payment confidence matters to both sides.

Work photos provide something concrete to judge, and availability is included in paid listings so discovery reflects who can take the job. The proposed payment flow uses Stripe Connect to hold payment until the work is done, connecting discovery, scheduling, and completion.

PROJECT 04 / 2026

Northern Utility Dispatch

Alpha

Oil, water & sewage dispatch for the North

Oil, water, and sewage services involve three people with different questions: a resident wants to know where a request stands, a dispatcher needs to assign the work, and a driver needs usable delivery details. This project brings those needs together for northern Quebec and Nunavut.

THE APPROACH

The workflow follows a request from resident to dispatcher to driver, with status and assignments carried through the handoff. Civic-number addresses shape the delivery information. The alpha demonstrates how the same job can be represented clearly for each role.

Supabase / PWA

PROJECT 05 / 2026

Q

In progress

Geo-fenced queueing for nightlife

Q began with a familiar frustration: standing outside a bar or club for thirty minutes in rain or snow. I wanted to improve the experience equally for the person waiting and the staff admitting them, with less outdoor waiting and faster entry.

THE APPROACH

The system under development uses geolocation and geo-fencing to manage a virtual queue. That separates holding a place from physically standing at the door. Live occupancy information supports the venue’s side of admission, while guests can wait somewhere more comfortable.

Geo-fencing / Mobile

PROJECT 06 / 2026

Alpha

Discover and track mineral deposits

As a certified minerals prospector, I wanted a useful place for anyone interested in prospecting to record and share discoveries. A find might be something to discuss with a team or something worth showing publicly; the person recording it should have that choice.

PROJECT 08 / 2026

SpennyBurger

Archived

Smash-burger pickup & payment portal

When I was cooking burgers in the North, I did not have a local phone number. I had Facebook, but I could not keep answering order messages while making the burgers myself. SpennyBurger grew out of that immediate conflict between taking orders and fulfilling them.

THE APPROACH

Customers place an order and pay through the portal without waiting for me to reply. A kitchen view lets me handle the order during service, then trigger a pickup text when it is ready. Each step removes a message I would otherwise have to stop cooking to answer.

PROJECT 09 / 2026

Invstar

Archived

Stock tracking & reorder planning for the North

I built Invstar for my own work as a department manager in northern Canada. I was tracking inventory with paper and memory, with stock scattered between a warehouse, sea cans, freezers, coolers, dry storage, and backrooms. Difficult logistics made mistakes expensive: ordering too much tied up stock and space; ordering too late meant running out before replenishment arrived.

THE APPROACH

Recording usage had to take less effort than remembering it. A single tap deducts a bag or unit from the running count. The system accounts for how much each unit holds, where it is stored, daily consumption, and supplier lead time to recommend when to reorder and how much. The count becomes an ordering decision.

Web

PROJECT 10 / 2026

MetalPRO Terminal

Archived

Bloomberg-style metals & minerals terminal

I built MetalPRO for my dad and me. We wanted to follow commodity prices, explore interesting metals and mining companies, and inform our investment research. Our interests stretch from precious metals to energy, rare earths, and strategic minerals.

THE APPROACH

The terminal groups those markets into keyboard-driven views. Live prices show current conditions, candlestick charts expose price history, and futures curves offer another view of the market. Bringing these perspectives into one console supports moving between a broad scan and a closer look.

Vanilla JS / HTML/CSS / Netlify Functions / Yahoo Finance
