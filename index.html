PROJECT 09 / 2026

Invstar

Archived

QSR inventory & ordering

A department-manager inventory system for remote operations. Track units across warehouses, sea cans, freezers, coolers, dry storage, and backrooms; tap when a unit is used; and get reorder guidance based on stock, daily use, and lead time.

THE PROBLEM

In the North, stock can be split between a warehouse, sea cans, freezers, coolers, dry storage, and a backroom—and paper and memory make both over-ordering and stockouts likely.

THE APPROACH

A department-manager inventory system that tracks units on hand, storage locations, pack sizes, daily usage, and lead times. Tap once when a bag or unit is used; Invstar keeps the running count and recommends when and how much to reorder.

Web

> ABOUT THE BUILDER

I am a builder and systems architect focused on the operational layer: how people move, pay, queue, book, dispatch, discover work, and keep track of what matters. I live and work in the remote North of Canada, where I work in retail management and build around the constraints I see firsthand—spotty connectivity, no card payments, civic-number addresses, field work, and people who should not need a manual. I am also a sound engineer and a certified minerals prospector, happiest panning for gold or looking at rocks. Through Presidia Corp, the problem-solving corporation, I turn real constraints into simple, durable tools that fit the place they are meant to serve.
