PROJECT 01 / 2026

Ride-booking for a remote Nunavik town

> ABOUT THE BUILDER

I am a builder and systems architect focused on the operational layer: how people move, pay, queue, book, dispatch, discover work, and keep track of what matters. I live and work in the remote North of Canada, where I work in retail management and build around the constraints I see firsthand—spotty connectivity, no card payments, civic-number addresses, field work, and people who should not need a manual. I am also a sound engineer and a certified minerals prospector, happiest panning for gold or looking at rocks. Through Presidia Corp, the problem-solving corporation, I turn real constraints into simple, durable tools that fit the place they are meant to serve.
